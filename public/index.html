How to use it

  1. Add deadlines

    Open My Deadlines and fill in the form: document type, due date, and any notes.

  2. See what's due

    Deadlines are sorted soonest first, with the days left or how many days late.

  3. Keep it current

    Change a deadline's status as you file, edit it if plans change, or delete it.

  4. Learn from others

    Browse tips by process, or share what you wish you had known.

There are no accounts. Your deadlines and tips are saved to this browser, so use the same browser each time.

Not legal advice. Tips are shared by students and are unverified. Always confirm with your DSO or a tax professional.
